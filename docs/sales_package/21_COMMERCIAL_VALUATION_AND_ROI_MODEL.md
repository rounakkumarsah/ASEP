## 3. Buyer Return on Investment (ROI) & Payback Period

### Scenario A: Global System Integrator (White-Label Deployment)
* **Fleet Deployment:** 500 Enterprise Developers using ASEP internally for client migration projects.
* **Productivity Gain:** 4.2 hours saved per developer per week (10.5% boost).
* **Weekly Savings:** $500 \times 4.2 \text{ hrs} \times \$65/\text{hr} = \$136,500/\text{week}$.
* **Annual Economic Value:** **$7,100,000 / year**.
* **Payback Period on $500k Acquisition:** **Under 4 Weeks (< 1 Month)**.

### Scenario B: AI SaaS Platform Rollup (Commercial Monetization)
* **Pricing Tier:** $299/mo (Team) and $1,499/mo (Enterprise Sovereign).
* **Year 1 Target:** 250 Team subscriptions + 35 Enterprise subscriptions.
* **Year 1 ARR:** $(250 \times 299 \times 12) + (35 \times 1499 \times 12) = \$897,000 + \$629,580 = \mathbf{\$1,526,580\text{ ARR}}$.
* **Net ROI Year 1:** **> 200% Cash-on-Cash Return**.
